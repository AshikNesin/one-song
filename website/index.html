One Song

One song. On repeat.

Every night, my kid wants the exact same lullaby. Not a playlist. Not a library. Just that one song, starting the moment I open the app. I built One Song because nothing else did exactly that.

Pick one audio file from your device. The app plays it continuously, loops it gaplessly, and keeps playing while your screen is locked or you're in another app. Set a sleep timer — five minutes, sixty, or anything between — and it stops quietly when the time is up.

No playlists. No libraries. No accounts. No ads. No subscriptions. The app remembers your song even if you move or rename the original file. Open it tomorrow and it starts playing immediately.

  • Instant play — opens and starts, no extra taps.
  • Loops forever — gapless, native repeat mode.
  • Sleep timer — 5 to 60 minutes, configurable default.
  • Background audio — plays while locked or in other apps.
  • Lock screen controls — play, pause, stop from the notification shade.
  • Remembers your song — cached so it never gets lost.

People use it for bedtime lullabies, rain sounds looped through the night, and that one track they can't stop listening to. The sleep timer handles the rest.
